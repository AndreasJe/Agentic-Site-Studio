3-2-1, and what isn't backed up here

Three copies, two kinds of storage, one off-site copy the servers can't delete (immutable storage). Two server rooms in the same building count as one site.
Not backed up: the staging and production servers (rebuilt from a release package plus config in git) and tokens (reissued after a restore). Release packages live inside the data folder, so they are covered.
A restore drill into the staging environment every quarter, and once before real use. It's on the Checks tab as a scheduled check.
"Download project" as a zip is for moving and sharing, not a backup.
